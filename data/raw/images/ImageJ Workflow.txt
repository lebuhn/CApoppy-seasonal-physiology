For the leaves, I had trouble getting the make binary feature to function accurately.
As a workaround, I went to Image>Adjust>Brightness/Contrast. I turned the brightness down and the contrast up,
so that the values on the left and right of the viewer read 108 and 240.
It looks like the minimum value needs to be around 110. The maximum value is more flexible.
